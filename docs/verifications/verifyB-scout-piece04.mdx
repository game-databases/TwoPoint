## Overall verdict

**PASS-WITH-CORRECTIONS.** The report's positive measurements are
exceptionally clean — every headline number, config value, enum, and
line anchor I attacked reproduced exactly, and the join-key contract
works end-to-end on first trace. But two of its load-bearing NEGATIVES
fail under a harder search, and both license site-facing behavior:
G4's "site must not invent grade cut-offs" is moot because the
cut-offs are client data (`Config_UISprite.Grades[]`), and G3's "zero
decay-rate carriers" is false because staff base rates are already
landed in `stubs/staff.jsonl` via `ECPCharacterAttributes` (with
`ECPStudent` covering club/relationship/self-study decay; only the
student core-11 carrier remains genuinely unfound). Corrections owed
before the report drives implementation: rewrite G3/G4 around the
measured carriers, fix the four numeric/name details (CalculateRoomScore
7 not ≥8; BudgetType 28 not 27; EStaffStat 31 constants not "23-stat";
Expelled events are `TPC.CharacterEvents.Student`), restate §7's
research-costs row as 209-with-costs-of-450, promote the
GradeMoneyRewards-zeros caveat, and add the InteractionDefinition /
upgrade-sink / course-points omissions to the gap ledger.
