## 1. Goal

The landed corpus holds complete *rows* and, since piece-02, complete
*relations*. What `extracted/logic/` still lacks (`.gitkeep` only) is the
third doctrine layer: **gameplay logic reconstructed from client data** —
progression chains, the economy's taxonomy and carriers, the grading
ladder, and the needs/decay constants — each emitted as a deterministic,
contract-pinned dataset with provenance down to the field path it was
read from.

This piece adds ONE entrypoint stage — `logic` — that derives, from
committed artifacts only, four dataset families:

1. **Course progression** — prerequisite chains over the 24-type
   taxonomy (incl. the 50 `PrerequisiteHasCourseUnlocked`
   course→course unlock edges resolved through the externals/pathId
   machinery), the 319-row module→room chain, course economics and
   ladders, and the attrition trigger set;
2. **Economy ledger** — the money taxonomy (28 `BudgetType` categories
   verbatim), finance configs with InitialBalance/fail-states, the
   Kudosh sources/sinks graph as a separate currency, course points as
   a third spendable, and research-point costs (209 cost-bearing rows);
3. **Grading** — the EGrade ladder, the `Config_UISprite.Grades[9]`
   Threshold table as DATA, per-term PassGrade, the
   `CourseAssessmentScoring` inputs, and the XP→score normalization as
   an **absent-with-ledger-row** artifact (never invented);
4. **Needs & decay** — staff `ECPCharacterAttributes` decay rates +
   init ranges, student `ECPStudent` rates, the student core-11 decay
   carrier as an absent-with-ledger-row set, and the
   `InteractionDefinition` family (630 rows) as the satisfaction layer.

Everything runs through the single entrypoint ([DR-2026-08-18-pipeline]):
`python run_all.py <game-dir> --only logic` must reproduce every
declared output byte-identically. Site/UI anything, native analysis
(R3: deferred, single known target named in §3 L3), stub re-ingestion
(R2: NOT bundled here), and PROOF/RELATIONS mutation stay out (§6).

**Hard law (R4, restated inline where it bites):** every formula on the
scout's not-data-driven list ships labeled
`provenance:"reconstructed-from-code"` + `buildId` if it ships at all;
**invented numbers are a launch-gate failure** (FRAMEWORK §8). Stage L5
enforces this mechanically — see §3 L5 and AC7.

### L5 — Invention guard + gap ledger

Two mechanical passes, both LOUD:

1. **Invention guard (R4 made executable).** Every emitted artifact is
   re-opened and every numeric leaf audited against its row's
   `evidence` citation: the cited source artifact + field path is
   re-read and the value must MATCH (float-exact for copied floats;
   integer-exact otherwise). Copied-and-matching numerics are the
   DEFAULT audited path and need NO inline label — they pass by
   citation alone; the guard counts them in the `copied` bucket. Three
   LABELED escape hatches exist for numerics that cannot be audited by
   direct citation, each visibly labeled IN the artifact:
   (a) `provenance:"reconstructed-from-code"` blocks (code-layout
   projections like `summaryColumns`) — checked for the label +
   buildId, not against a payload; (b) derived aggregates carrying
   `method:"derived-arithmetic:<expr>"` + the input citations (counts,
   splits, min/max of a domain) — the guard RECOMPUTES each expression
   from its cited inputs rather than trusting the string; a
   non-recomputable or mismatching result = **exit 1**, so this hatch
   cannot launder an invented number; (c) explicit
   `null`/absent-with-ledger representations (L3/L4 above). A numeric
   satisfying NONE of these routes — copied-but-mismatching, unlabeled,
   mis-derived — is a **failure**, naming artifact + path. This is the
   launch-gate enforcement of "invented numbers fail".
