### R4.0 — The premise of the open question is REFUTED by data

G6 asked "which generation instantiates at runtime", presuming the two
base families are successive generations of one config set. They are
not. [AP] resolving every `_ScenarioV2` GUID through the catalog shows
a TOTAL, exception-free split of all 27 resolvable configs by address
path:

| Generation (bundle family) | Rows | Scenario addresses wired | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| `configs-levels-prefabs` | 12 playable + blank | `Assets/Data/Game/Levels/Final/Part1_Early … Part3_LateGame/...` — every row a campaign STORY scenario | campaign-mode carrier |
| `configs_assets_all` | 9 | `Assets/Data/Game/Levels/Remix/Remix_1 … Remix_10/...` — every row a Remix/event scenario | remix-mode carrier |
| `dlc-space-configs` | 4 | `DLC1_Space/Game/Levels/Final/{SpaceportCity,LaunchPad,MoonBase}` + `Test/SpaceLevel` | DLC1 carrier |
| `dlc-ghost-configs` | 2 | `DLC2_Ghost/Game/Levels/LevelScenarioV2_Ghosts` (story) + `Remix_Ghost/…_Remix_V2` | DLC2 story + remix |

The "Robotics/Spy disagreement" is a story-vs-remix difference on the
same campus scene, not version drift; the Ghosts ×2 pair is the same
pattern inside the DLC2 family. There is no "older variant" to record
as history: **both families are contemporaneous, live, mode-scoped
wiring in buildId 20226581.** The orchestrator preference ("pin the
generation actually shipping … record older variants as historical
notes") is therefore honored in its spirit — never silent — and
corrected in its premise: pinning one family "canonical" would declare
live mode data historical. REFUTED as unnecessary and wrong.

(The campus-level stub GUID route was checked first and is a dead end
[AP]: none of the 17 `campus-levels` LevelConfig GUIDs resolve through
the catalog — those rows are dev/test configs ("Blank Level",
"Mark's Test Scenario", IL3 videos), not progression wiring.)

### R4.1 — Canonical generation: NONE pinned; question downgraded

- No `canonical` flag is emitted while
  `loadassets_read.json.readStatus == "inconclusive-from-dumpcs"`.
  Both rows of every twin pair ride `variantOf` — the spec's documented
  fallback is RATIFIED as terminal policy for this buildId, not a
  waiting state.
- The conclusive body-read (decompile `d__532.MoveNext` at RVA
  **0x8D8D10**, VA 0x1808D8D10 — see F18 for the corrected citation)
  is **downgraded from gating to optional corroboration**: it can only
  confirm loading MECHANICS the data-side split already answers for
  every site-facing purpose. It routes to the orchestrator as an
  out-of-stage follow-up and is recorded honestly in
  `loadassets_read.json.unblock`.
- The one-field upgrade path (`canonical:true`) stays reserved in the
  schema exactly as Rev 2 designed it; it is NOT expected to be needed.
  If ever exercised, it requires a spec revision citing new evidence,
  never an implementation choice.

### R4.2 — How variants are recorded

Separate FIRST-CLASS ROWS in the closed four-family vocabulary, linked
by `variantOf`; NEVER collapsed into historical notes, prose
annotations, or "preferred row" picks. Annotations (derived, method-
stamped, e.g. the story/remix reading above if the Documentator adopts
it) may ride rows but may not replace or reclassify them. Basis:
extraction Principle zero (everything reachable ships), the
drift-wins seed discipline, and R4.0 — collapsing live mode data into
notes is data destruction.

### R4.3 — G10: scenario-record counts vs LevelConfig PlotCounts

Neither wins, because they measure DIFFERENT OBJECTS: scenario plot
records are the authored inventory of one scenario (including
unbuilt/merge variants); `PlotCount` is the authored build-target
number on ONE mode-scoped config asset. Policy:

1. `plots.jsonl` / `scenarios.jsonl` counts are the PLANNER-SUBSTRATE
   truth (ruling R1) — the campus layout surface derives from them.
2. `levels.jsonl.plotCount` ships `{value, generation}` verbatim per
   config row; consumers who show a SINGLE per-campus plot number must
   either derive it from scenario records or name the exact
   `(levelId, generation)` row they read. Silent single-number picks
   are a defect.
3. Known divergences (LaunchPad 18 records vs 11; Mitton 38 vs 9 —
   both [AP]-confirmed) stay DRIFT-checked seeds; no reconciliation is
   invented in the stage.
4. The `PlotBuildManager` merge/build code-read stays routed outside
   stage maps, likewise downgraded to explanatory follow-up: emission
   no longer depends on it.

### R4.4 — Effect on the residual round

No schema migration: Rev 2's `variantOf` + closed-enum design absorbs
this ruling as-is once F14 restores a workable identity key. The
Documentator SHOULD record R4.0's family↔mode table as a derived
