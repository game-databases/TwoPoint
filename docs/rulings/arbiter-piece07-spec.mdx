### R2 — `metricRule` vocabulary: ACCEPTED, with two structural consequences declared

The frozen predicates are sound and uniform:
`byteIdenticalToEn := non-empty text(locale) == text(en)`;
`identicalAndDeDiffers := byteIdenticalToEn AND text(de) != text(en)`;
de stays comparator for all 13 rows; counters range over HELD keys.
Freezing ONE comparator for all rows is what makes ru≡en an instance of
a uniform table rather than a special case — the right cut, and the
residual-4 emission turns the 301−297 delta into data.

Two degenerate rows follow from the freeze and are DECLARED EXPECTED,
not bugs (no AC contradicts them):

- the **de** row has `identicalAndDeDiffers` structurally **0**
  (self-comparison: `text(de) != text(en)` can never hold when
  `byteIdenticalToEn(de)` does);
- the **en** row has `byteIdenticalToEn` == all held keys (trivially
  true), and its `identicalAndDeDiffers` reads as "keys where de differs
  from en" — informative, just semantically different from the other
  rows.

CodeWriter emits them under the same rule; TestWriter may assert these
structural facts on fixtures; a future reviewer may not file either as
a defect. The L2 name-edge metricRule ("first fieldPath containing
'name', sorted `(srcKind,srcId,fieldPath,dstId)` order") is exactly the
rule verifyA reconstructed to exact agreement on five kinds — accepted
as-is, with config's tie-break honesty handled by F9's pinned DRIFT
note.
