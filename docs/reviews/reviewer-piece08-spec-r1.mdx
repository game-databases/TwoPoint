## Findings

### F1 — MAJOR — AC4 sizing bands and their F16 anchors are not derivable from the fact base and contradict each other

Location: §2 F16; §6 AC4; §9 client-gated sanity floors.

What's wrong. Three connected defects:

1. F16's titles anchor — "**≈249 B/doc rich schema**, 0.94 MB @ **3,793
   docs**" — has an unexplained denominator: 3,793 appears nowhere else in
   the spec or the fact base, and it contradicts §9's own floor ("en shard
   doc count ≥ 7,000"). Under the S2 membership rule every expanded-universe
   member ships an en doc (localized edges resolve in en because the
   registry↔en key diff is 0 and stage 4 emits zero empty texts; dev/literal
   material is English), so the en shard lands near the 10,200 planning
   floor. At 249 B/doc that is ≈2.5 MB — **above** AC4's titles ceiling of
   1.6 MB — while the PINNED slim shape `{kind,id,t}` (~100 B/doc) lands
   ≈1.0 MB, inside the band. The band's pass/fail therefore depends on
   which "titles projection" schema a CodeWriter believes.
2. F16's full-doc anchor "1.74 MB" carries no doc-count denominator at all;
   at its implied ≈459 B/doc × ~10.2k docs the full en shard is ≈4.7 MB —
   outside AC4's [1.0, 3.5] MB — while lean emitters (~300 B/doc) land
   inside. Same flippable-band defect.
3. AC4's Σ ≤ 28.4 MB "≤3× entity-relevant source law" divides the wrong
   universe: 9.45 MB covers only the 6,526 registry-referenced keys, but the
   shipped docs additionally serialize plain-string literals (×1,456), dev
   fallback strings (3,874 rows), joined item titles (≤2,005) and course
   conventions — source text that is BY CONSTRUCTION outside the
   denominator. The law can fail on a correct implementation.

Fix shape. Prototype-measure one locale (en) with the pinned schemas and
re-derive the bands from that measurement; state F16's doc-count
denominators explicitly; either re-state the Σ law over the actual doc-source
text universe or demote it to a DRIFT-tracked informational metric; delete
the "rich schema" titles anchor so `{kind,id,t}` is the only reading. Until
then AC4 cannot be checked blind — the exact failure mode pre-ruling 2
exists to prevent.

### F3 — MAJOR — the `LocalisedName*` wildcard is load-bearing in three seed sets but never expanded; the naive reading trips the exit-1 assertion

### F4 — MAJOR — §S2's membership iff-list omits convention/curated resolution, so the 28 real courses go missing from all 12 non-en shards under the strict reading

Location: §S2 "Membership rule"; §S3.2; §3 constraint 1.

What's wrong. Membership is defined iff "≥1 NON-EMPTY text RESOLVED IN L —
a localized term edge with text in L's table, or an `mTerm` key resolving
in L, or (any locale) a plain literal." Measured: the 28
`TPC.CourseDefinition` rows have ZERO `entity_locale` edges (all 41 course
edges are `Marketing_Course_*`), so their only name material is
convention/curated — a basis the membership list does not include, even
though the name-resolution chain and `basis` enum both carry
`convention|curated`. The family keys (`Courses/Courses/KnightSchool_Name`,
…) exist in all 13 tables, so a convention resolution resolves per-locale
naturally; under the iff-list as written the resolved course names ship in
the en shard only and up to 25 × 12 (doc, locale) pairs vanish from exactly
the per-locale surface this piece calls its differentiator (FRAMEWORK §2.4
spirit; DR-2026-08-20-locale-urls routing).

Fix shape. Add the fourth clause — "or a `convention`/`curated` resolution
whose `termKey` resolves in L" — and pin that family/tail matching runs
against each locale's table (keys are locale-independent; only the text
differs), with unresolved-in-L rows behaving like any other membership miss.
