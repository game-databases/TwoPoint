### What this means for planning

- The corpus is small (3.5 GiB, 176 bundles incl. DLC) — full harvest is
  cheap compared to every prior pack on this host.
- Content is NOT encrypted or custom-packed beyond Unity AssetBundles +
  LZ4/LZMA block compression: no depot-level encryption observed.
- The Addressables catalog bundle (1.7 MiB) is a machine-readable index of
  every addressable key → bundle/address mapping: it is the natural first
  extraction artifact and the backbone of the relink layer.
- `configs_assets_all`, `configs-app/common/metagame/levels-prefabs`
  bundles are where the logic layer will live; `localisation_*` are the
  locale overlays (§4).
