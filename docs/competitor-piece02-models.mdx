## 1 — `fandom`: the community's full relationship schema

Source: Two Point Campus Wiki, `two-point-campus.fandom.com`
(`twopointcampus.fandom.com` 301s there), MediaWiki 1.43.9. HTML is
challenge-walled (scout-002: curl 403, crawler-UA 402), but **api.php
answers plainly** — the whole pull ran through
`action=query`/`list=categorymembers`/`prop=revisions&rvprop=content`.

Pulled: complete category list (282 cats, `allcategories-p1.json`),
members of 16 structural categories incl. the FULL Items category (500 +
337 = 837 pages, reconciles against category size), and 91 pages of raw
wikitext in six batches (rooms ×20, courses ×15, items ×24, hub/meta
pages, plus the three load-bearing infobox templates themselves).

### Entity kinds the community maintains

course · room · item · campus · teacher/staff-kind (Teacher, Assistant,
Janitor) · student archetype · trait/skill · assignment · club · event ·
research project · Kudosh/money economy · wall/floor decoration variants
· DLC/content-axis groupings. Against our provisional
[spec.md](../spec.md) entity set, three kinds are community-load-bearing
but absent from our list: **assignment**, **club**, **event** — flagged
below as matrix additions, not noise.

### The infobox field map (= their join keys)

From `wikitext-A-templates-staff.json` (templates) + instance pages:

| Infobox | Fields | Edges they encode |
|---|---|---|
| `Template:Infobox Room` | game, type, placement, effect(s), staff, course, capacity, unlock, size, price | room→room-class, room→placement-class, room→staff-kind, room→campus(unlock), room→capacity/size/price |
| `Template:Infobox Course` | game, unlock, cp, fees, difficulty, duration, room[], archetype[] | course→campus(unlock), course→room[] (**the site's core join**), course→archetype, course→difficulty/duration class |
| `Template:Infobox Item` | game, unlock, filter[], effect (+level1/2/3 tiers), kudosh, price, value, room[] | item→room[] (placement), item→unlock-source (**research link or campus or Kudosh**), item→effect-axis with typed power value, item→item-class filter, item→Kudosh cost |
| `Template:Infobox Campus` (instance: Mitton University) | climate, unlock(=previous campus), course, intake, balance | campus→climate, campus→campus (**linear progression chain**), campus→featured course, campus→starting intake/balance |

Body sections extend the infoboxes: course pages carry
`{{CourseStaff}}`, `{{CourseRooms}}`, `{{CourseSelfStudy}}` (per-year
class→room→item chains, e.g. Wizardry self-study uses Library/Potions
Room with Magic Cauldron items); item pages carry an **Assignments**
wiki-table mapping assignment-description → courses (Super Computer →
Internet History, Space Academy, Money Wangling…).

### Category taxonomy as relation axes

The 282 categories encode four orthogonal classification systems, each a
candidate join dimension:

1. **Effect axes** — `Increases/Decreases <Need>` pairs (Attractiveness,
   Energy, Entertainment, Happiness, Healthiness, Hunger, Hygiene,
   Learning Rate, Movement Speed, Teaching Skill, Temperature, Thirst,
   Toilet Comfort): the community's item→need-effect model, categorically
   indexed.
2. **Power axes** — Learning/Medical/Marketing/Pastoral/Training/
   Treatment/Research Power: room-scoped numeric buffs (infobox stores
   the number: `{{Learning Power|10}}`).
3. **Progression classes** — Easy/Medium/Hard/Very-Hard Difficulty
   Course; N-Year Courses duration classes; campus ladder (12 base + 3
   Space Academy + 1 School Spirits + 3 Medical School campuses).
4. **Content axes** — per-DLC categories (Space Academy DLC 86 pages,
   School Spirits DLC 38, Medical School DLC 72) and Amazon-Prime/Kudosh
   bundle item sets.

## 3 — `steam-guides`: player-derived mechanics chains

Source: Steam Community guides hub, appid 1649080. The bare `/guides/`
shape scout-002 probed returns a JS-driven shell (0 guide links) — but
that was the URL shape, not a wall: with the AI-crawler UA against the
paginated browse shape
(`/guides/?browsefilter=trend&numperpage=30`), server-rendered listing
markup appears (9 guide rows). Four model-bearing guides pulled in full
(ids/titles in `steam-guides/PROVENANCE.md`; index in
`_f2-guide-index.tsv`).

Model extracted (34 rows in `model.jsonl`, cardinality claims marked):

- **Room capacity 8** students; recommended course intake multiples of 8;
  **one teacher manages ~16 year-1 students** — the classroom-economy
  triangle (room↔course↔staff) with numbers attached.
- **1 dormitory bed : 5 students**, beds × prestige → accommodation
  rating → rent: a derived economy chain our happiness/rent simulator
  (tools-plan T3/T4) must reproduce from client data.
- **Item effects stack within-room only, diminishing returns** ("+1%
  Learning Power" Brain Jar example, ~2300 jars ⇒ sub-1-day training) —
  confirms the room-scoped buff semantics behind J4 and gives a
  falsifiable prediction for client-side formula extraction.
- Staff **negative-trait list** (Bin-Blindness, Bottomless Pit,
  Dry-Mouth, Gross, Lollygagger, Weak Bladder) and skill % deltas
  (Private Tuition +5%/level vs course qualification +10%/rank, stacking)
  — staff-side logic-layer targets.
- Club recruitment stands **optionally** take an Assistant (tooltip says
  required — a UI-vs-truth divergence worth checking in client data).
- **Campus ladder**: base levels 1–12 named in order; Space Academy DLC
  = Universe City, Cape Shrapnull, Cheesy Heap: Delta-Rye; School Spirits
  DLC = Lifeless Estate; **Medical School DLC = Lake Tumble, Molten Rock,
  Pointy Peak** — independent corroboration of J11's third content axis.
- Per-campus objective lists (goal→build room/place item) — a goal-tree
  edge family matching the `configs-metagame` expectations.

Caveat recorded per row: these are player-derived figures — treat as
`cardinalityClaim` hints, never measured truth; where a client-derived
value exists it wins and the guide row becomes confirms-hard evidence
only.
