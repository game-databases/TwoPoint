# Stage 3 — harvest + media catalogue

Every export filename embeds `<bundle-stem>_<signed-int64 pathId>` —
60,582 of 167,069 manifest rows hold NEGATIVE path_ids, so the sign is
contract, not decoration (Rev 6 lesson; V-S10 enforces the grammar).

Stage 3's media duty is **census**, not decoded completion. Media-catalogue
rows reconcile with the carved census per class (V-R3) and cab objects with
the census total (V-R2). Those rows are **inputs to mandatory downstream
decoding** under `[DR-2026-08-29-media-scope-amended]` (every text/image and
every non-location model under 50 MB). A catalogue hit is not an extracted
image or model. Entity-web subset evidence lives in
`extracted/media/MEDIA-EXPORT.md`; class-universe coverage stays in
`extracted/MEDIA-CATALOGUE.md`.
