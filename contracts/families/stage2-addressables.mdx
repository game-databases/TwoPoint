# Stage 2 — addressables trio + catalog-mini-report sidecar

catalog.json (397,431,200 B) is the heavy artifact: default runs NEVER
open it — catalog-level pins verify against the persisted emit-time
sidecar `addressables/catalog-mini-report.json` (six-key schema, V-S8),
and `--scan-catalog` streams catalog.json once to re-derive it
byte-for-byte (V-I2 agreement, AC6). The duplicate key and the null-bundle
population are documented exceptions — see
[exceptions.mdx](exceptions.mdx).
