axes-carrier row); evidence keysets are exact per method. Matrix pairs[]
are row-major over the node universe; Σ cardinality.edges == pair rows
(V-R1). The GUID bridge replays BIT-EXACT from sibling artifacts through
the pure seam `relink_util.run_guid_bridge` off the sidecar's full
guidIndex (V-R8, CLIENT-GATED). Exit 2 is the designed steady state until
the [ledger map](../ledger-map.mdx) closes.
